Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

FreeFollowersTik respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in with TikTok, we may receive information that TikTok makes available through the permissions you authorize.

This may include:

  • TikTok user ID.
  • Display name.
  • Profile information.
  • Profile avatar.
  • Profile statistics when authorized.

2. TikTok Authentication

FreeFollowersTik uses TikTok authentication to allow users to securely sign in to the platform.

We do not request or store your TikTok password. Authentication is handled through TikTok's authorization system.

3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Authenticate your account.
  • Create and maintain your FreeFollowersTik account.
  • Display your TikTok profile information.
  • Provide creator promotion features.
  • Manage coins and transactions.
  • Prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Improve platform security and performance.

4. Coins and Transactions

We store information necessary to maintain your virtual coin balance and transaction history.

This may include coin amounts, transaction types, transaction references, and timestamps.

5. Payment Information

If you purchase coins, payment processing may be handled by a third-party payment provider.

We do not intend to store your full card number or card security code on FreeFollowersTik servers. Payment providers process payment information according to their own privacy policies and security requirements.

6. Cookies and Sessions

FreeFollowersTik may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain secure login sessions and protect the authentication process.

Authentication cookies may contain secure session identifiers. These identifiers are used to recognize your authenticated session.

7. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the platform, process payments, provide infrastructure, maintain security, or comply with legal requirements.

8. Third-Party Services

FreeFollowersTik may use third-party services including TikTok, hosting providers, databases, analytics services, and payment providers.

These third parties may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account records, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

11. Your Choices

You may stop using FreeFollowersTik at any time. Depending on the available account features, you may also request deletion or correction of your information.

12. Children's Privacy

FreeFollowersTik is not intended for children who are below the minimum age permitted to use the applicable services and third-party platforms.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a revised update date.
